# Khalid Kebir (revert influencer): Identity, Biography and Conversion Story

## Who is Khalid Kebir, and is there one clearly identifiable Muslim revert influencer by this name (or a close spelling)?

### Takeaway
The only influencer named "Khalid Kebir" I could find is **Khalid Kebir, known online as "Irish Habibi"** (@irish.habibi). He is a London-based content creator about Muslim life and culture who describes his heritage as Irish and North African (Algerian flag on his profiles), and he also runs a business advisory firm. I am **moderately confident (about 70%)** that he is the person meant. The name matches, and he is a Muslim-culture creator with a large following. However, **no source I could verify calls him a "revert" or tells his conversion story.** His official bio cites "Irish and North African heritage", which fits being born into a mixed Irish/Algerian family as well as it fits being a convert. I found no other person called Khalid Kebir, Khaled Kebir, Khalid Kabir or Khalid Kebeer who is described as a revert.

### Inferences
- "Khalid Kebir" is probably the right spelling; the alternative spellings turn up nothing relevant. The name, the Muslim-life content and the "blonde Muslim in mosques" theme make Irish Habibi the most likely intended person.

The official bio's "Irish and North African heritage" and "third-culture upbringing", together with the Algerian flag, could equally mean he was born into a mixed Irish/Algerian Muslim family.
