# Khalid Kebir ("Irish Habibi"): Public Reception, Impact and Controversies

> Scope note (read first): Searches for "Khalid Kebir", "Khaled Kebir" and "Khalid Kabir" with revert/convert terms found **no source describing a person of this name as a revert or convert to Islam**. The only clearly identifiable public figure is **Khalid Kebir, known online as "Irish Habibi" (@irish.habibi)**, a London-based creator of Irish and Algerian/North African heritage. The available sources describe his heritage and Muslim-life comedy but **do not describe a conversion**. One of his own video titles ("Being a Blonde Muslim: My Journey in Mosques") is about being *asked* when he converted. That suggests he may be a heritage (born) Muslim who gets taken for a convert, but this is an inference, not verified. The report writer should not call him a "revert" unless another researcher found a primary source (for example, his own video saying he took the shahada). Overall, there is very little independent coverage of his reception.

## What do Reddit, forums, comment sections, and media say about them?

### Takeaway
Almost nothing independent exists. No Reddit threads, forum discussions or mainstream or Muslim media profiles about Khalid Kebir / Irish Habibi turned up. What exists is self-descriptions (a speaker profile, social bios), a celebrity-bio aggregator, his own TikTok posts, and one foreign-language reshare of a viral Gaza-related clip.

### Inferences

## Have there been any controversies, criticisms, or public disputes?

### Takeaway
No documented controversy, theological criticism, authenticity dispute or monetisation criticism was found. The only "notable moment" found is a viral clip mocking an Israeli claim during the Gaza war, which drew attention in Turkish media. No reports of backlash against him were found.

### Cited Findings

### Gaps
