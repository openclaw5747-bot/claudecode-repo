# Khalid Kebir: Social Media Presence and Content

Research date: 10 November 2026. Public web sources only. Instagram and TikTok blocked direct fetches (HTTP 429 / empty page), so the platform figures below come from search-result snippets and third-party pages, not live profile reads.

**IDENTITY FLAG (read first):** Searches for "Khalid Kebir", "Khaled Kebir" and "Khalid Kabir" with revert, convert, dawah, TikTok and YouTube turned up **no creator described as a Muslim revert or convert** under any spelling. The only Khalid Kebir found who works as a creator is **"Irish Habibi" (@irish.habibi)**. A TRT speaker profile links that name and handle to Khalid Kebir. He is described as having **Algerian/North African and Irish heritage**. No source found says he converted to Islam. His Algerian heritage suggests he may have been raised Muslim, but no source confirms this either way. The "revert influencer" description in the brief is therefore **unverified and may be wrong**. Other people with similar names (the Irish convert Khalid Kelly, the Algerian politician Rabah Kebir, a Nuremberg entrepreneur "Khalid (El) Kebir") are not the same person.
