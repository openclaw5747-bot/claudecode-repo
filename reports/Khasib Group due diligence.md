# Khasib Group due diligence

*Researched 10 Nov 2026. Claims were each checked by three independent verifiers.*

## Summary

Khasib Group is a real, active brand: it has a working website (khasibgroup.com), a published report and a conference profile. However, it is not a registered UK company under that name. Companies House returns no company called "Khasib" or "Khasib Group", now or under a previous name. The site names Khalid Kebir as Founder and Principal Advisor and calls the firm a London-based strategic advisory for Global Muslim markets. A Khalid Mohamed KEBIR (born June 1996, British, London SE8) is the sole director of two other UK companies: VIADUNYA LTD (17129169, incorporated 31 Mar 2026, software and web portals) and WITH 786 LTD (15331438, incorporated 6 Dec 2023, clothing retail). Either could be the legal vehicle behind the brand, but no record links them to it. The firm's flagship product, "Cultural Currency 2026" (March 2026), is described as a free 54-page report with 23 contributors and a foreword by Baroness Manzila Uddin. It headlines a $7.4T Global Islamic Economy. TRT Next lists Kebir as a speaker, founder of Khasib Group and lead author of the report. Almost all evidence comes from the firm itself or from bios it probably supplied. There is no independent press coverage, no named client, no confirmed partnership, and no registration details on the website.

## Findings

### 1. No UK company named 'Khasib Group' (or any 'Khasib' name) is on the Companies House register. So there is no incorporation date, company number, registered address or director record for an entity under that name.

**Confidence:** high · **Vote:** 3-0 (claims 0, 7, 23)

A company search for 'khasib' and 'khasib group' on 10 Nov 2026 returned 'There are no results that match your search.' One verifier notes that this search also covers previous company names. The firm could still trade under another legal name or as an unincorporated trading name.

Sources: <https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/search/companies?q=khasib>, <https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/search/officers?q=khalid+kebir>

### 2. Companies House lists one closely matching officer, Khalid Mohamed KEBIR: born June 1996, British, living in England, identity verified, with correspondence address Flat 62 Lapwing Tower, Taylor Close, London SE8 5UH. He has exactly two current appointments, and neither company is called Khasib Group.

**Confidence:** high · **Vote:** 3-0 (claims 1, 6, 7)

The officer page says 'Total number of appointments 2': VIADUNYA LTD (17129169) and WITH 786 LTD (15331438), both active, with no resignations. Other Kebir officers in the search results are different people. No record proves that this officer is the 'Irish Habibi' creator, though the name and London location match.

Sources: <https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/search/officers?q=khalid+kebir>, <https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/officers/QGshMqXsf27HDkN8La8dEKo22sQ/appointments>

### 3. VIADUNYA LTD (17129169) is an active private limited company incorporated on 31 March 2026, with SIC codes 62012 (software development) and 63120 (web portals). Khalid Mohamed Kebir has been its sole director since incorporation. Its first accounts are due for the period to 31 March 2027.

**Confidence:** high · **Vote:** 3-0 / 2-1 (claims 2, 3, 24)

The company overview and officers pages show 1 officer and 0 resignations. The registered office is the same Lapwing Tower address. Its incorporation in March 2026 coincides with the report's launch, but that link is inferred, not documented.

Sources: <https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/17129169>, <https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/officers/QGshMqXsf27HDkN8La8dEKo22sQ/appointments>

### 4. WITH 786 LTD (15331438) is an active private limited company incorporated on 6 December 2023, with SIC code 47710 (retail sale of clothing). Khalid Mohamed Kebir has been its sole director since incorporation.

**Confidence:** high · **Vote:** 3-0 (claims 4, 5)

Companies House overview and officers pages: 1 active officer, 0 resignations, same SE8 address. It appears to be a separate clothing venture, and nothing shows a link to Khasib Group.

Sources: <https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/15331438>

### 5. Khasib Group's website presents it as a London-based strategic advisory for Global Muslim markets that helps 'brands, investors, and institutions enter and expand across the $7.4 trillion Global Muslim Economy'. The About page names Khalid Kebir as Founder and Principal Advisor, described as a 'third-culture strategist shaped by Irish and North African roots'. He is the only team member named.

**Confidence:** high · **Vote:** 3-0 (claims 8, 11)

Quoted directly from the live site on 10 Nov 2026. The homepage names no team members, though it quotes contributors from outside the firm (Omar Khaliel, Naquib Mohammed, Nohman Ahmed, Arfah Farooq, Shaher Abbas). The About page also mentions a '5M Framework'. All of this is the firm describing itself.

Sources: <https://www.khasibgroup.com/>, <https://www.khasibgroup.com/about>

### 6. The website gives no company registration number, incorporation date, street address or phone number. The only contact details are contact@khasibgroup.com, 'Based in London, UK, serving clients globally', and hours of Mon–Fri 10am–6pm GMT. It says client work is confidential and client outcomes are not published.

**Confidence:** high · **Vote:** 3-0 (claims 8, 13, 14, 17)

Searching the raw HTML for 'registered', 'company no' and 'Companies House' found nothing. UK companies are generally expected to show their registered name and number on their websites, so the absence matters, especially given that no 'Khasib' entity is registered.

Sources: <https://www.khasibgroup.com/>, <https://www.khasibgroup.com/about>, <https://www.khasibgroup.com/contact>, <https://www.khasibgroup.com/global-archetypes>

### 7. The firm's services and products are a 30-minute 'market assessment' consultation that maps a brand to the report's frameworks and identifies the most viable entry point; the 'Cultural Currency 2026' report; and public 'Archetype Briefs' and 'Region Briefs' under an 'Intelligence' menu.

**Confidence:** high · **Vote:** 3-0 (claims 18, 19)

These come from the site's navigation and contact page. No pricing is published, and the site does not show that any client has used the service.

Sources: <https://www.khasibgroup.com/contact>, <https://www.khasibgroup.com/cultural-currency-2026>

### 8. 'Cultural Currency 2026: The KHASIB Report on Global Muslim Markets' was published in March 2026. According to the firm, it is a free 54-page download with 23 contributors across 8 sectors, drawn from '20+ executive interviews', with a foreword by Baroness Manzila Uddin (a real member of the House of Lords).

**Confidence:** medium · **Vote:** 3-0 (claims 9, 12, 15, 16)

The description is the same across several pages of the firm's own site. Nobody downloaded the PDF to check the page count, contributors or foreword. No independent coverage or catalogue entry was found, and nothing confirms that Baroness Uddin wrote the foreword.

Sources: <https://www.khasibgroup.com/>, <https://www.khasibgroup.com/about>, <https://www.khasibgroup.com/global-archetypes>, <https://www.khasibgroup.com/cultural-currency-2026>

### 9. The report's headline figures are: Global Islamic Economy $7.4T; Islamic Finance $4.93T (+8.8%/yr); Halal Food $1.43T (+6.3%/yr); Muslim Travel $217B (+12.1%/yr); Modest Fashion $327B (+5.8%/yr); UK Muslim contribution £70B+ a year; and Islamic Economy growth of '10%+ vs 1–2%' for Western markets. It names three structural shifts: the wealth transfer to 'Gen M', the 'Tayyib standard', and the adult daughter as 'Chief Household Officer'.

**Confidence:** medium · **Vote:** 2-1 (claim 10)

The website credits these figures to the report. The sector figures look like they come from DinarStandard's State of the Global Islamic Economy data, but the site does not cite a source for them. The 10%+ growth claim is well above common third-party estimates of about 5–7%. The claim accurately reports what the report says, not whether the figures are true.

Sources: <https://www.khasibgroup.com/>

### 10. TRT Next lists Khalid Kebir as a speaker. His bio there calls him a London-based market intelligence expert, founder of Khasib Group (a strategic advisory firm connecting institutions with the Global Islamic Economy) and lead author of Cultural Currency 2026. It says he is known online as 'Irish Habibi' with over 1.2M followers, and that he advises C-suite executives, private equity firms and policymakers.

**Confidence:** medium · **Vote:** 3-0 / 2-1 (claims 21, 22)

This is the only third-party page that links Kebir, Irish Habibi, Khasib Group and the report. It is a conference bio the speaker probably supplied, so it shows that TRT featured him, not that his claims were independently checked.

Sources: <https://next.trt.net.tr/speakers/khalid-kebir/>

### 11. Khasby Group Ltd (khasbygroup.com) is an unrelated company in Tema, Ghana, working in haulage, marine engineering, construction and similar fields, and should not be confused with Khasib Group. Searches also turned up other similar but unrelated names: Khas Group Ltd, Hasbi Group Ltd, Khasab Ltd, Hasib Ltd, Khashab Ltd, Al-Khalid Group (Kuwait), and the Omani footballer Mohamed Khasib.

**Confidence:** high · **Vote:** 3-0 (claim 20, plus verifier notes)

Khasby's site says it is 'Located in Ghana's Industrial Port City of Tema' and never mentions the Islamic economy, London or Kebir. The other look-alike names came up in verifiers' web and Companies House searches.

Sources: <https://khasbygroup.com/>

## Caveats

Nearly all the substantive information (positioning, founder, services, the report and its figures) comes from the firm's own website. The one third-party source, TRT Next, is a speaker bio the speaker probably supplied. No independent press coverage, client list, case study, partnership announcement or review of the report was found. The officer Khalid Mohamed KEBIR matches the reported founder by name and London location, but no record ties him to the Irish Habibi creator, and neither of his companies is formally linked to the Khasib Group brand. Companies House does not check the information filed with it. The PDF was not downloaded, so the page count, contributor list and Baroness Uddin's foreword are unconfirmed, and there is no evidence she endorsed the report. The report's market figures look partly borrowed from third-party data such as DinarStandard and include a growth claim (10%+) that looks high. Possible red flags: the website shows no registered company name or number, VIADUNYA LTD was incorporated very recently (March 2026) and has not yet filed accounts, and the firm publishes no client outcomes. These are reasons for due diligence, not evidence of wrongdoing. Everything here was checked on 10 Nov 2026 and may change, for example if a company is registered or renamed.

## Open questions

- Which legal entity, if any, contracts and invoices under the Khasib Group name: VIADUNYA LTD, WITH 786 LTD, or Kebir as a sole trader?
- Can the report's 23 contributors and the Baroness Uddin foreword be confirmed by the contributors themselves or by checking the PDF?
- Is there independent coverage of Khasib Group or Cultural Currency 2026 (trade press, Islamic finance media, recordings of the TRT Next event), or any named clients or partners?
- Where do the report's market figures come from, and how is the 10%+ annual growth claim supported?
